== Roadmap

What trystero does not port yet, and what it does not build itself yet
(<<trystero-differences>> gives each feature left out with its reason):

. *Media.* Audio and video tracks and streams, and Trystero's `@_stream` and
`@_signal` actions with renegotiation. In the browser they are
the page's `MediaStreamTrack` objects. Natively they would be libdatachannel's
RTP tracks, whose encoding, decoding and capture are the application's. What
that needs was measured: libdatachannel keeps only weak references to
tracks, answers with every codec offered unless the answer is pruned, and
echoes the offerer's `msid` into the answer. Media will be an interface of
webcpp::webrtc's, which the backends' WebRTC is.
. *The port to Trystero 0.27.0.* On the branch `port/trystero-0.27`:
Phase 2, identities and the handshake signed with ECDSA P-256, verified
users and `maxUsers`, and the interop against 0.27.0, which merges the port;
then Phase 3, each a change of its own, `makeState` and `makeUserState`,
`roles`, and `scale`.
. *Trystero's other features.* `requestMany`, the progress and the
cancelling of sends and requests, passive rooms, and the other strategies,
among them MQTT and BitTorrent trackers.
