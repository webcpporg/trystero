== Roadmap

What trystero does not port yet, and what it does not build itself yet
(<<trystero-differences>> gives each feature left out with its reason):

. *Media.* Audio and video tracks and streams, and Trystero's `@_stream`,
`@_track` and `@_signal` actions with renegotiation. In the browser they are
the page's `MediaStreamTrack` objects. Natively they would be libdatachannel's
RTP tracks, whose encoding, decoding and capture are the application's. What
that needs was measured: libdatachannel keeps only weak references to
tracks, answers with every codec offered unless the answer is pruned, and
echoes the offerer's `msid` into the answer.
. *Trystero's other features.* Request actions (`kind: 'request'`),
decisions on incoming transfers and their progress, the application's own
handshake, passive rooms, and the other strategies, among them MQTT and BitTorrent trackers.
. *libsecp256k1 from webcpp's own build.* libsecp256k1 is still found where
b2's command line or the superproject's `.local/` says. Native OpenSSL and
libdatachannel already build from pinned `third_party/` sources
(<<trystero-building>>).
