== Roadmap

What trystero does not port yet, and what it does not build itself yet
(<<trystero-differences>> gives each feature left out with its reason):

. *Media.* Audio and video tracks and streams, and Trystero's `@_stream` and
`@_signal` actions with renegotiation. In the browser they are
the page's `MediaStreamTrack` objects. Natively they would be libdatachannel's
RTP tracks, whose encoding, decoding and capture are the application's. What
that needs was measured: libdatachannel keeps only weak references to
tracks, answers with every codec offered unless the answer is pruned, and
echoes the offerer's `msid` into the answer. Media will be an interface of
webcpp::webrtc's, which the backends' WebRTC is.
. *Trystero 0.27.0's plugins.* Each a change of its own, on an
interoperable library: signed records and shared state, `makeState` and
`makeUserState`; roles and moderation, `roles` with its bans and role
certificates; and `scale`, with its relayed peers and their pairwise keys.
. *Trystero's other features.* `requestMany`, the progress and the
cancelling of sends and requests, the receive and request decisions
answered later, with 0.27.0's bounds on those pending (128 receive
decisions, 8 of one peer, 64 concurrent requests of one peer), passive
rooms, and the other strategies, among them MQTT and BitTorrent trackers.
. *What webcpp::crypto does not provide yet.* A public wipe of a buffer of a
size known only at run time: trystero wipes the password's copies and the
buffers of an import with its own `detail::wipe` until webcpp::crypto has
one.
