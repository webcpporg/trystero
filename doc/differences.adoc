== Differences from Trystero

trystero ports the part of Trystero 0.26.0 that rooms of messages need: the
Nostr strategy, the signaling, the password handshake, the action wire and
the room's API. Where it keeps a feature, it keeps Trystero's wire and its
defaults; where it bounds what a peer or a relay can make a room hold, it
says so in <<trystero-protocol-bounds>>, since Trystero JS bounds none of it.
These features of Trystero are not ported, each for its reason:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Not ported | Why

| Audio and video: `addStream`, `removeStream`, `addTrack`, `removeTrack`,
  `replaceTrack`, `onPeerStream` and `onPeerTrack`, with the actions
  `@_stream`, `@_track` and `@_signal` and their renegotiation
| The largest part left, on the roadmap
  (<<trystero-roadmap>>): in the browser, the page's `MediaStream` objects;
  natively, libdatachannel's RTP tracks, whose capture, encoding and
  decoding would be the application's. A room ignores a frame of one of
  these actions.

| Request actions, `kind: 'request'`: `request`, `requestMany` and
  `onRequest`
| Not ported yet: an application answers a message with another, to its
  sender's id.

| `onReceive`, which accepts or declines an incoming transfer; the progress
  callbacks `onProgress` and `onReceiveProgress`; and cancelling a send with
  an `AbortSignal`
| Not ported yet: a room accepts every transfer for an action with a
  receiver within `max_receive_bytes`, as Trystero does when no `onReceive`
  is set, and reports a transfer only when it completes.

| `onPeerHandshake`, a handshake of the application's own
| Not ported yet: the password handshake, which runs on the same internal
  action, `@_hsdata`, is.

| Passive rooms, `passive: true`
| Not ported yet: a room always announces itself.

| One connection shared by every room of an app between two peers
| Not ported yet: each room makes its own connections, and ignores a frame
  that names another room's token.

| The BitTorrent, MQTT, Firebase, Supabase, IPFS and WebSocket relay
  strategies
| Each is a strategy of its own to port; Nostr's public relays need no
  account and no server of the application's own.

| `getRelaySockets`, `pauseRelayReconnection`, `resumeRelayReconnection`,
  `manualReconnection` and `warnOnRelayFailure`
| Not ported yet: a room reconnects with its backoff, and reports what its
  relays do through its log.

| `trickleIce: false`, `turnConfig` and `rtcPolyfill`
| Candidates are always trickled, as Trystero's Nostr strategy does by
  default; `ice_servers` names every STUN and TURN server, in place of
  Trystero's defaults; and the backend is what a polyfill would be.
|===

No example of trystero runs under the build, since each needs the public
relays or a browser, so none has a twin in Trystero's language, and the
interop lane, not a twin, compares trystero with Trystero
(<<trystero-testing-lanes>>).
