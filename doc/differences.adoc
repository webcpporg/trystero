[#trystero-differences]
== Differences from Trystero

trystero ports the part of Trystero 0.26.0 that rooms of messages need: the
Nostr strategy, the signaling, the password handshake, the action wire and
the room's API. Where it keeps a feature, it keeps Trystero's wire and its
defaults; where it bounds what a peer or a relay can make a room hold, it
says so in <<trystero-protocol-bounds>>, since Trystero JS bounds none of it.
These features of Trystero are not ported, each for its reason:

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Not ported | Why

| Audio and video: `addStream`, `removeStream`, `addTrack`, `removeTrack`,
  `replaceTrack`, `onPeerStream` and `onPeerTrack`, with the actions
  `@_stream`, `@_track` and `@_signal` and their renegotiation
| The largest part left, on the roadmap
  (<<trystero-roadmap>>): in the browser, the page's `MediaStream` objects;
  natively, libdatachannel's RTP tracks, whose capture, encoding and
  decoding would be the application's. A room ignores a frame of one of
  these actions.

| Request actions, `kind: 'request'`: `request`, `requestMany` and
  `onRequest`
| Not ported yet: an application answers a message with another, to its
  sender's id.

| `onReceive`, which accepts or declines an incoming transfer; the progress
  callbacks `onProgress` and `onReceiveProgress`; and cancelling a send with
  an `AbortSignal`
| Not ported yet: a room accepts every transfer for an action with a
  receiver within `max_receive_bytes`, as Trystero does when no `onReceive`
  is set, and reports a transfer only when it completes.

| `onPeerHandshake`, a handshake of the application's own
| Not ported yet: the password handshake, which runs on the same internal
  action, `@_hsdata`, is.

| Passive rooms, `passive: true`
| Not ported yet: a room always announces itself.

| The BitTorrent, MQTT, Firebase, Supabase, IPFS and WebSocket relay
  strategies
| Each is a strategy of its own to port; Nostr's public relays need no
  account and no server of the application's own.

| `getRelaySockets`, `pauseRelayReconnection`, `resumeRelayReconnection`,
  `manualReconnection` and `warnOnRelayFailure`
| Not ported yet: a room reconnects with its backoff, and reports what its
  relays do through its log.

| `trickleIce: false`, `turnConfig` and `rtcPolyfill`
| Candidates are always trickled, as Trystero's Nostr strategy does by
  default; `ice_servers` names every STUN and TURN server, in place of
  Trystero's defaults; and the backend is what a polyfill would be.
|===

[#trystero-differences-behaviour]
=== Where a ported feature behaves otherwise

[cols="2,3,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Behaviour | Trystero JS | Here | Why

| Scope of sharing and of the self id | the page: one `selfId` for every app and room, one manager per strategy | an `app` object: its self id and its connections | no static state; several peers in one process; an id across apps is a program's choice (`app_config::self_id`) and never reaches the wire of another app

| A peer kicked from a room, or that kicked us, over a live connection | bound again by its next signal or presence | bound again at a rebinding time, 11 s doubling to 5 min; the lower id refuses it meanwhile and keeps its frames | a wrong password in one room would otherwise loop reports and announcements without end; one refusing side cannot alternate with another

| Reuse over a live connection | binds on any signal of that peer id | binds only a peer that advertised the room's token itself, and by reuse only on their first meeting over that connection | a forged announcement must not bind an innocent peer

| Announcements after a peer leaves | restarted after every leave | restarted only when no live connection to that peer remains | over a live connection, rebinding goes through the connection

| Presence after binding | sent when a room registers | also sent again when a room binds by reuse or at its rebinding time | it makes the other side, Trystero JS included, bind at once

| An answer to an earlier challenge | checked against the current challenge only | accepted when it is correct for any of the last 8 challenges sent since the last completed handshake | a late answer of an ended binding is honest and proves the password; failing it reports a false error

| Frames after the higher id's own kick | kept for the next binding | dropped until the higher id sends presence | they belong to the ended binding; flushed into the next one, they would answer or be answered in its place

| Joining a room already joined | the same room | `error::already_joined` | a `room` is a handle whose destruction leaves

| Rooms per app | unbounded; a peer advertising a 65th is dropped by the other side | `join` refuses the 65th, counting rooms in their farewell | the 65th would cost every connection of the app

| A connection whose last room was kicked | idle 123,333 ms | closed at once | a bound: a failed peer holds no connection, as today

| Relay sockets and the Nostr key | shared by the rooms of the page, topics batched per socket | per room, as today | not part of this decision; invisible to peers; shared through the app in a later change

| Relay and ICE configuration | the first room's relays; each negotiating room's RTC configuration | the app's, for every room | one configuration for what the rooms share

| `idle_timeout` | test-only (`_test_only_sharedPeerIdleMs`) | a configuration member, Trystero's default | tests and programs that close sooner

| Frames waiting for a bound room's handler | 64, 10 s, then the connection drops | no such state | a binding's delivery is set when it is made

| Renegotiation and media over a shared connection | routed through a control room, streams owned per room; a connection with any media is destroyed when its last room goes | not ported | media is on the roadmap (<<trystero-roadmap>>)

| Passive rooms | presence only for active rooms | not ported | as today

| Crossed duplicate connections | first registered wins at each side; crossed, both lose and meet again | the same | matching Trystero's rule is what keeps a {cpp} and a Trystero JS side agreeing

|===

*`on_peer_join` does not replay the joined peers.* Trystero calls a newly
assigned `onPeerJoin` with each peer already joined. A room reports a join
from its executor, after the call to `join` has returned, so a
handler set right after `join` sees every join; `peers()` lists the
joined peers for a handler set later.

*`peers()` lists ids.* Trystero's `getPeers` maps each id to its
`RTCPeerConnection`. A connection is the backend's, a page's object in the
browser and libdatachannel's natively, and the room does not hand it out.

*A send to a peer that has not joined fails.* Trystero warns on the console
and resolves; `async_send` completes with `error::no_such_peer`, since an
operation that fails says so with its error code.

No example of trystero runs under the build, since each needs the public
relays or a browser, so none has a twin in Trystero's language, and the
interop lane, not a twin, compares trystero with Trystero
(<<trystero-testing-lanes>>).
