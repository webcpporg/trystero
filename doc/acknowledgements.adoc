== Acknowledgements

trystero ports the wire protocol of https://github.com/dmotz/trystero[Trystero]
0.26.0, Copyright (c) 2021 Dan Motzenbecker, so that its rooms are
Trystero's rooms. It was written with Trystero's source open: its protocol
constants, its message layouts, its default relays in their order, with the
shuffle that derives an app's relays from its id, and its default STUN
servers come from it. Trystero is distributed under the MIT License, whose
text and copyright notice are in `LICENSE-TRYSTERO.txt`, at the root of the
library, exactly as Trystero 0.26.0 ships them. That adds no licence to
trystero's own code, which is under the Boost Software License alone.

The BIP-340 signatures of the Nostr events are
https://github.com/bitcoin-core/secp256k1[libsecp256k1]'s, by the Bitcoin
Core contributors, under the MIT License. The native backend's WebRTC is
https://github.com/paullouisageneau/libdatachannel[libdatachannel]'s, by
Paul-Louis Ageneau, under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
