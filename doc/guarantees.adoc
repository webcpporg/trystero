[#trystero-guarantees]
== Guarantees

What trystero promises, and the test that holds each promise, which fails
when the promise breaks. A guarantee keeps its number, and the anchor named
after it, so a comment of the code or of a test cites it by that anchor:
`+(doc: #trystero-invariant-3)+` sends its reader to the third.
`b2 libs/trystero/doc`, which builds this page, fails on a citation that
names no anchor of it. The tests are those of <<trystero-testing>>.

. [[trystero-invariant-1]]*The wire is Trystero's.* A {cpp} peer,
in the browser or native, and Trystero JS peers in one room join, exchange
text, JSON and binary messages, files both ways with metadata, and pings,
leave, and form a mesh, with either side offering; each verifies the
other's signatures before it joins it, sees its peer and user ids, and
refuses another password, a third user past `max_users` and a forged user
signature. _Held by_ the `interop` lane, `interop_browser` on emscripten and
`interop_native` natively, against Trystero 0.27.0 itself in headless
Chrome.
. [[trystero-invariant-2]]*Every protocol value equals Trystero JS's.*
Topics, ids, room keys, room auth tokens, the relay shuffle, sealed text,
topic kinds, Nostr event ids, inline and chunked action frames and control
frames are what Trystero 0.27.0's own functions compute from the same
inputs, byte for byte; the envelopes, room tokens and presence the branch
keeps are 0.26.0's. _Held by_ `crypto_layer` and `crypto_layer_release`,
natively and on emscripten, against the vectors
`test/oracle/record_0_27.mjs` recorded from 0.27.0's own code, sealed texts
opened as 0.27.0 sealed them among them; and by `protocol`, against the
values Trystero 0.27.0 writes for the `oracle` lane, which fails when
Trystero would write other values than those committed.
. [[trystero-invariant-3]]*A peer with another password never joins a
protected {cpp} app's rooms.* Its initial signals do not open under the
room's keys, derived from the app's password, so it obtains no shared
connection into that domain, and the room reports `decrypt_failed`, at most
once a minute per peer and stage. Every
room of that app uses the same immutable password. This promise concerns
the {cpp} app's admission; an upstream JS page that mixes passwords retains
its one-way handshake exposure (<<trystero-protocol-limitations>>). _Held
by_ `room` (`the_same_password_joins_and_another_does_not` and
`decrypt_failed_is_reported_once_a_minute`), `platform_room`
(`another_password_never_joins`), and the interop's `passwords` scenario,
with either side offering.
. [[trystero-invariant-4]]*A payload larger than a frame travels in chunks,
both ways.* It is offered, accepted and sent in chunks of at most 16,370
bytes, and arrives whole, from either peer. _Held by_ `room`
(`large_payloads_travel_in_chunks_both_ways`, 100 KB each way), and the
file transfers of the `interop` lane.
. [[trystero-invariant-5]]*Every allocation of an app and its rooms goes through their
resources,* but what <<trystero-allocation-outside>> lists: from its
construction until its `io_context` has drained, neither the global
`operator new` nor the C library sees an allocation, while its rooms read
and write their JSON texts and exchange text, binary and JSON payloads of
the `std::pmr` types and `webcpp::document::pmr::document`; over the
platform backend's relays, natively a message adds nothing, and in a page
each event of a relay's `WebSocket` adds embind's string of the event's
type, two for a message.
Natively OpenSSL's cipher context of each seal and open is outside, as
listed, and counted. _Held by_ `allocation`, natively and on emscripten; and
for the relays,
`platform_websocket_allocation` natively and
`platform_page_relay_allocation` in a page, which counts exactly what is
listed.
. [[trystero-invariant-6]]*No unit test reaches beyond the loopback.* The
rooms of the unit tests meet over the memory relays, the native tests' peer
connections bind to 127.0.0.1 and gather their candidates there alone, and
the WebSocket test talks to a server of its own on 127.0.0.1. _Held by_ the
container lanes, `gcc-14` and `clang-linux-18`: every unit test, built with
GCC 14 and with clang 18 against `libstdc{plus}{plus}`, passes in a Linux
container that has no network but the loopback (`docker run` with the
network `none`).
. [[trystero-invariant-7]]*A room refuses a replayed or reflected offer or
answer.* A sealed offer or answer it made, or acted on, that comes back to it
as a peer's changes nothing (<<trystero-protocol-replay>>); one replayed or
reflected as another kind, or for another offer, does not open, its AAD
being another's. _Held by_ `room` (`a_replayed_answer_does_not_open`,
`a_replayed_offer_is_refused`, `a_reflected_offer_is_refused_as_an_answer`,
`a_reflected_offer_is_refused_as_an_offer` and
`a_reflected_answer_does_not_open_as_an_answer`).
. [[trystero-invariant-8]]*A relay that stops reading is dropped.* Once the
text held unsent for it would pass 4 MiB, the connection is dropped, and
reports one close, after which the room reconnects with its backoff, as it
does to any relay that closes. _Held by_ `platform_websocket`
(`a_relay_that_stops_reading_is_dropped_at_the_queue_bound`, a close before
2000 texts of 64 KiB, and one only) natively, and on emscripten, with
exceptions and without them, by `platform_page_websocket_bound`, where the
page's `WebSocket` holds what is unsent, which also checks that it never
holds more than 4 MiB, and `platform_page_websocket_late_events`, where the
session's queue does, which checks one close and no message after it; the
reconnection by `room`
(`a_relay_that_closes_at_once_is_tried_less_and_less_often`).

. [[trystero-invariant-9]]*One connection per remote peer per app is shared
by its rooms,* on the branch until Phase 2 retires 0.26.0's sharing
(<<trystero-design-phase-1>>). Each room keeps its own membership and actions on that
connection. _Held by_ `shared`, cases 1 to 3, and the interop's first shared
scenario.
. [[trystero-invariant-10]]*A room's departure leaves the app's other rooms
joined.* They continue sending over the same connection. _Held by_
`shared`, case 8, and shared interop scenario 5, with either side leaving.
. [[trystero-invariant-11]]*Different passwords share no {cpp} app
connection or presence.* Protected rooms use a separate app with a
distinct self id; a peer of a passwordless app gains no binding to that
protected app by echoing tokens. _Held by_ the password-domain cases of
`shared` and shared interop scenario 4.
