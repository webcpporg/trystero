[#trystero-guarantees]
== Guarantees

What trystero promises, and the test that holds each promise, which fails
when the promise breaks. A guarantee keeps its number, and the anchor named
after it, so a comment of the code or of a test cites it by that anchor:
`+(doc: #trystero-invariant-3)+` sends its reader to the third.
`b2 libs/trystero/doc`, which builds this page, fails on a citation that
names no anchor of it. The tests are those of <<trystero-testing>>.

. [[trystero-invariant-1]]*The wire is Trystero 0.26.0's.* A {cpp} peer,
in the browser or native, and Trystero JS peers in one room join, exchange
text, JSON and binary messages, files both ways with metadata, and pings,
leave, and form a mesh, with either side offering. _Held by_ the `interop`
lane, `interop_browser` on emscripten and `interop_native` natively, against
Trystero 0.26.0 itself in headless Chrome.
. [[trystero-invariant-2]]*Every protocol value equals Trystero JS's.*
Topics, room tokens, the relay shuffle, sealed text, Nostr event ids, inline
and chunked action frames, control frames, envelopes and presence are what
Trystero's own functions compute from the same inputs, byte for byte. _Held
by_ `protocol`, against the values the `oracle` lane has Trystero 0.26.0
write, and by the `oracle` lane, which fails when Trystero would write
other values than those committed; and by `native_sealer`, which opens what
Trystero JS sealed with Web Crypto.
. [[trystero-invariant-3]]*A peer with another password never joins.* Its
signals do not open, and its handshake fails, so neither side reports it
joined. _Held by_ `room` (`the_same_password_joins_and_another_does_not`),
`native_room` (`another_password_never_joins`), and the wrong-password
scenario of the `interop` lane.
. [[trystero-invariant-4]]*A payload larger than a frame travels in chunks,
both ways.* It is offered, accepted and sent in chunks of at most 16,370
bytes, and arrives whole, from either peer. _Held by_ `room`
(`large_payloads_travel_in_chunks_both_ways`, 100 KB each way), and the
file transfers of the `interop` lane.
. [[trystero-invariant-5]]*Every allocation of a room goes through its
resource,* but what <<trystero-allocation-outside>> lists: from its
construction until its `io_context` has drained, the global `operator new`
sees only the room's JSON storage counter, and the C library nothing. _Held
by_ `allocation`, natively and on emscripten.
. [[trystero-invariant-6]]*No unit test reaches beyond the loopback.* The
rooms of the unit tests meet over the memory relays, the native tests' peer
connections bind to 127.0.0.1 and gather their candidates there alone, and
the WebSocket test talks to a server of its own on 127.0.0.1. _Held by_ the
container lanes, `gcc-14` and `clang-linux-18`: every unit test, built with
GCC 14 and with clang 18 against `libstdc{plus}{plus}`, passes in a Linux
container that has no network but the loopback (`docker run` with the
network `none`).
. [[trystero-invariant-7]]*A room refuses a replayed or reflected offer or
answer.* A sealed offer or answer it made, or acted on, that comes back to it
as a peer's changes nothing (<<trystero-protocol-replay>>). _Held by_ `room`
(`a_replayed_answer_is_refused`, `a_replayed_offer_is_refused`,
`a_reflected_offer_is_refused_as_an_answer`,
`a_reflected_offer_is_refused_as_an_offer` and
`a_reflected_answer_is_refused_as_an_answer`), and the replay and
reflection scenarios of the `interop` lane.
. [[trystero-invariant-8]]*A relay that stops reading is dropped.* Once the
text held unsent for it would pass 4 MiB, the connection is dropped, and
reports one close, after which the room reconnects with its backoff, as it
does to any relay that closes. _Held by_ `native_websocket`
(`a_relay_that_stops_reading_is_dropped_at_the_queue_bound`, a close before
2000 texts of 64 KiB, and one only) natively, and `browser_websocket_bound`
on emscripten, with exceptions and without them, which also checks that the
socket never holds more than 4 MiB; the reconnection by `room`
(`a_relay_that_closes_at_once_is_tried_less_and_less_often`).
